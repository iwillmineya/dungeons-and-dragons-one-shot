Description

The Fugue Plane is a desolate, gray expanse suspended between the material world and the outer planes. It is the destination of mortal souls upon death, where they are sorted by psychopomps and sent to their final destinations based on their lives and beliefs. This plane is shrouded in mystery, despair, and the weight of countless souls.

Notable Locations

The Gray Wastes

An endless, barren landscape where lost souls wander in confusion and despair.

Kelemvor's Throne

The seat of divine judgment where the god of death determines souls' final fates.

Gates to Beyond

Mystical portals leading to the various afterlife realms and outer planes.

NPCs & Encounters

Kelemvor

The god of death itself, judge and custodian of souls. Few mortals encounter him and live.

Psychopomps

Celestial servants who guide souls and enforce Kelemvor's will on this plane.

Lost Souls

Spirits trapped between worlds, sometimes dangerous, often tragic.

Adventure Hooks

  • Journey to this plane to recover a stolen soul
  • Negotiate with Kelemvor himself for a life owed
  • Guide a wrongly judged spirit to its proper destination
  • Stop a soul thief operating between worlds
  • Escape the Fugue Plane after an unexpected death

Characteristics

Appearance: A desolate realm of gray stone and mist, devoid of color and life. The sky is perpetually overcast, and the ground extends infinitely in all directions.

Danger Level: EXTREME - This plane is the domain of death itself. Only the most powerful or divinely protected can survive here without being claimed.

Notable Features: No living creatures naturally inhabit this plane. Death magic is amplified. Resurrection magic is significantly weakened.

⚔️ Who You're Going to Be Fighting

Enemy/Encounter 1: Kelemvor, God of Death

Type: Deity

CR: 11

HP: 210

AC: 18

Ability Scores

STR 20 (+5) | DEX 14 (+2) | CON 22 (+6) | INT 16 (+3) | WIS 18 (+4) | CHA 18 (+4)

Passive Abilities

Aura of the Final Reckoning: Creatures that start their turn within 10 ft. take 7 (2d6) necrotic damage unless they succeed on a DC 16 Con save.

Immutable Judgment: Cannot be charmed, stunned, or magically put to sleep.

Magic Resistance: Advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects.

Actions

Multiattack: Makes three attacks with Greatsword of Finality.

Greatsword of Finality: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 15 (2d6 + 5) slashing plus 10 (3d6) necrotic damage. If target below half HP, additional 7 (2d6) radiant damage.

Sentence of the Grave (Recharge 5–6): Target within 60 ft. must make DC 16 Con save. Failure: 45 (10d8) necrotic damage, speed 0 until end of next turn. Success: half damage. If reduced to 0 HP, cannot be revived for 1 minute.

Reaction Abilities

Balance the Scales (1/day): All creatures within 30 ft. must make DC 16 Wis save. Failure: 27 (6d8) radiant damage and restrained by spectral chains until end of next turn. Success: half damage, not restrained.

Legendary Actions (3 per round)

Spectral Strike: Makes one Greatsword attack.

Grave Step: Teleports up to 30 ft. to an unoccupied space.

Soul Weight (Costs 2 Actions): One creature within 30 ft. must succeed on DC 16 Wis save or become frightened until end of next turn.

⚡ Phase 2: The Veil Thins (At 100 HP)

Kelemvor sheds divine light; shadows writhe around him.

• Aura increases to 15 ft.

• Gains resistance to all damage except force.

• Immediately uses Sentence of the Grave (no recharge).

📋 Encounter Notes

For 6 Level 5 players: HARD encounter.

Someone may drop to 0 HP. If party is tactical, they win. If they clump up, they suffer.

If struggling: Reduce Aura damage to 1d6, remove resistance to nonmagical weapons.

If steamrolling: Add 2 spectral knights (CR 3 each), increase necrotic damage by 1d6 per hit.
