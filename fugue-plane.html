💀 Fugue Plane (Death)

The realm between life and the afterlife

← Back to Map

Description

The Fugue Plane is a desolate, gray expanse suspended between the material world and the outer planes. It is the destination of mortal souls upon death, where they are sorted by psychopomps and sent to their final destinations based on their lives and beliefs. This plane is shrouded in mystery, despair, and the weight of countless souls.

Notable Locations

The Gray Wastes

An endless, barren landscape where lost souls wander in confusion and despair.

Kelemvor's Throne

The seat of divine judgment where the god of death determines souls' final fates.

Gates to Beyond

Mystical portals leading to the various afterlife realms and outer planes.

NPCs & Encounters

Kelemvor

The god of death itself, judge and custodian of souls. Few mortals encounter him and live.

Psychopomps

Celestial servants who guide souls and enforce Kelemvor's will on this plane.

Lost Souls

Spirits trapped between worlds, sometimes dangerous, often tragic.

Adventure Hooks

  • Journey to this plane to recover a stolen soul
  • Negotiate with Kelemvor himself for a life owed
  • Guide a wrongly judged spirit to its proper destination
  • Stop a soul thief operating between worlds
  • Escape the Fugue Plane after an unexpected death

Characteristics

Appearance: A desolate realm of gray stone and mist, devoid of color and life. The sky is perpetually overcast, and the ground extends infinitely in all directions.

Danger Level: EXTREME - This plane is the domain of death itself. Only the most powerful or divinely protected can survive here without being claimed.

Notable Features: No living creatures naturally inhabit this plane. Death magic is amplified. Resurrection magic is significantly weakened.
