Description

Beyt Charith is a prosperous merchant city situated in fertile valleys where multiple trade routes converge. The city thrives on commerce and is known for its hospitality, fine goods, and cosmopolitan atmosphere. Traders from distant lands meet here to exchange goods and information.

Notable Locations

Grand Bazaar

A sprawling marketplace where anything can be bought or sold.

Merchant's Guild

The governing body of trade, wielding considerable power in the city.

Caravanserai

A fortified inn providing shelter and safety for traveling merchants.

NPCs & Encounters

Guild Master

A shrewd merchant who controls much of the city's economic activity.

Caravan Masters

Experienced traders always hiring guards and adventurers.

Street Merchants

Vendors ranging from honest to deceptive, each with their own tales.

Adventure Hooks

  • Protect a valuable trade caravan
  • Investigate smuggling operations
  • Retrieve stolen merchant goods
  • Mediate disputes between merchant factions

🗺️ Epic Quest: Cleanse the Realm

Objective: Kill all of the monsters who have taken over these cities

A darkness has spread across the realm. Powerful creatures have seized control of key settlements. The Merchant's Guild has issued a bounty on behalf of all civilized lands to rid these cities of their monstrous occupiers.

Locations with Enemies to Defeat:

Ashen Crown Mountains

Enemy: Aboleth (CR 10)

An ancient abomination dwelling in the mountain depths, enslaving those who encounter it.

Fugue Plane (Death)

Enemy: Kelemvor, God of Death (CR 11)

The god of death itself has somehow manifested on this plane. A challenge beyond mortal comprehension.

Highlands of Ephrah

Enemy: Invisible Stalker (CR 6)

An unseen terror stalking the highlands, striking from the shadows and leaving scholars in fear.

Migdal-Or

Enemy: Beholder - Tyrannoeil (CR 13)

A powerful aberration has claimed the great citadel. Its many eyes command terrible power.

Mourning Woods

Enemy: Chimera (CR 6)

A horrific multi-headed beast terrorizes the ancient forest and its ranger inhabitants.

Rewards: Glory, riches, and the gratitude of the realm await those brave enough to accept this monumental task.

⚔️ Who You're Going to Be Fighting

Enemy/Encounter 1: Succubus

Type: Medium Fiend (Shapechanger), Neutral Evil

CR: 4 (1,100 XP)

HP: 66 (12d8 + 12)

AC: 15 (natural armor)

Speed: 30 ft., fly 60 ft. | Proficiency Bonus: +2

Senses: Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15 | Languages: Abyssal, Common, Infernal, Telepathy 60 ft.

Ability Scores

STR 8 (-1) | DEX 17 (+3) | CON 13 (+1) | INT 15 (+2) | WIS 12 (+1) | CHA 20 (+5)

Skills: Deception +9, Insight +5, Perception +5, Persuasion +9, Stealth +7

Resistances & Immunities

Damage Resistances: Cold, Fire, Lightning, Poison; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks

Traits

Telepathic Bond: The fiend ignores range restriction on telepathy when communicating with a charmed creature. The two don't need to be on the same plane of existence.

Shapechanger: Can polymorph into a Small or Medium humanoid, or back into true form. Without wings, loses flying speed. Other than size and speed, statistics remain the same. Equipment isn't transformed. Reverts to true form if it dies.

Actions

Claw (Fiend Form Only): +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 10 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.

Charm: One humanoid within 30 ft. must succeed on DC 15 Wis save or be magically charmed for 1 day. Charmed target obeys verbal or telepathic commands. If target takes harm or gets suicidal command, can repeat save. Target is immune after successful save for 24 hours. Only one target charmed at a time.

Draining Kiss: Kiss a charmed or willing creature. Target makes DC 15 Con save, taking 32 (5d10 + 5) psychic damage on fail (half on success). Hit point maximum reduced by damage taken until long rest. Dies if maximum reaches 0.

Etherealness: Magically enters the Ethereal Plane from Material Plane or vice versa.
