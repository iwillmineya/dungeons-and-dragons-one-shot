Description

The Highlands of Ephrah are known for their rolling hills, lush green valleys, and ancient temples that dot the landscape. This region was once the heart of a grand civilization, and traces of their culture can still be found among the ruins. The highlands are home to various settlements and a thriving trade network.

Notable Locations

Grand Temple

A massive structure at the highlands' center, still partially intact and in use.

Scholar's Academy

An institution dedicated to preserving knowledge and training new mages.

Trading Post

A bustling center of commerce connecting multiple trade routes.

NPCs & Encounters

High Priestess

Leads the temple and wields considerable political influence in the region.

Master Scholars

Expert mages and historians who pursue forgotten knowledge.

Merchant Caravans

Traveling merchants always seeking adventurers for protection.

Adventure Hooks

  • Protect a merchant caravan from bandits
  • Investigate strange magical disturbances near the temple
  • Retrieve a stolen artifact for the Academy
  • Escort scholars to dangerous archaeological sites

⚔️ Who You're Going to Be Fighting

Enemy/Encounter 1: Invisible Stalker

Type: Medium Elemental, Neutral

CR: 6 (2,300 XP)

HP: 104 (16d8 + 32)

AC: 14

Speed: 50 ft., fly 50 ft. (hover) | Proficiency Bonus: +3

Senses: Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 18 | Languages: Auran, Understands Common but doesn't speak it

Ability Scores

STR 16 (+3) | DEX 19 (+4) | CON 14 (+2) | INT 10 (+0) | WIS 15 (+2) | CHA 11 (+0)

Skills: Perception +8, Stealth +10

Resistances & Immunities

Damage Resistances: Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks

Damage Immunities: Poison

Condition Immunities: Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious

Traits

Invisibility: The stalker is invisible.

Faultless Tracker: Given a quarry by its summoner, it knows direction and distance to its quarry as long as both are on the same plane. Also knows summoner's location.

Actions

Multiattack: Makes two slam attacks.

Slam: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage.
