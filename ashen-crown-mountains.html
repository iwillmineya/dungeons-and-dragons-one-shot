⛰️ Ashen Crown Mountains

Towering peaks shrouded in mist and ancient magic

← Back to Map

Description

The Ashen Crown Mountains rise majestically above the surrounding lands, their peaks often shrouded in grey mist. Ancient fortifications dot the slopes, remnants of a civilization long past. The mountains are said to hold great treasures and terrible secrets buried within their depths.

Notable Locations

Mountain Peaks

The highest summits reach into the clouds, creating a natural barrier to the north.

Ancient Ruins

Crumbling towers and fortifications hint at a powerful civilization.

Hidden Caves

Numerous caves and caverns honeycomb the mountainside, home to various creatures.

NPCs & Encounters

Mountain Hermit

An old sage who knows the secrets of the mountains. Rumor has it they possess ancient knowledge.

Goblin Tribes

Various goblin settlements inhabit the lower slopes, often feuding with each other.

Stone Golems

Constructs left behind by the ancient civilization, still guarding their posts.

Adventure Hooks

  • Retrieve a lost artifact from the ruins at the mountain's peak
  • Stop goblin raids on the surrounding settlements
  • Discover the fate of a missing expedition
  • Uncover the secrets of the ancient civilization
