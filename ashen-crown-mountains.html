Description

The Ashen Crown Mountains rise majestically above the surrounding lands, their peaks often shrouded in grey mist. Ancient fortifications dot the slopes, remnants of a civilization long past. The mountains are said to hold great treasures and terrible secrets buried within their depths.

Notable Locations

Mountain Peaks

The highest summits reach into the clouds, creating a natural barrier to the north.

Ancient Ruins

Crumbling towers and fortifications hint at a powerful civilization.

Hidden Caves

Numerous caves and caverns honeycomb the mountainside, home to various creatures.

NPCs & Encounters

Mountain Hermit

An old sage who knows the secrets of the mountains. Rumor has it they possess ancient knowledge.

Goblin Tribes

Various goblin settlements inhabit the lower slopes, often feuding with each other.

Stone Golems

Constructs left behind by the ancient civilization, still guarding their posts.

Adventure Hooks

  • Retrieve a lost artifact from the ruins at the mountain's peak
  • Stop goblin raids on the surrounding settlements
  • Discover the fate of a missing expedition
  • Uncover the secrets of the ancient civilization

⚔️ Who You're Going to Be Fighting

Enemy/Encounter 1: Aboleth

Type: Large Aberration, Lawful Evil

CR: 10 (5,900 XP)

HP: 135 (18d10 + 36)

AC: 17 (natural armor)

Speed: 10 ft., swim 40 ft. | Proficiency Bonus: +4

Senses: Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 20 | Languages: Deep Speech, Telepathy 120 ft.

Ability Scores

STR 21 (+5) | DEX 9 (-1) | CON 15 (+2) | INT 18 (+4) | WIS 15 (+2) | CHA 18 (+4)

Saves: CON +6, INT +8, WIS +6 | Skills: History +12, Perception +10

Traits

Amphibious: Can breathe air and water.

Mucous Cloud: Surrounded by transformative mucus. Creatures touching it or hitting within 5 ft. must make DC 14 Con save or become diseased for 1d4 hours, breathing only underwater.

Probing Telepathy: If a creature communicates telepathically, the aboleth learns its greatest desires.

Actions

Multiattack: Makes three tentacle attacks.

Tentacle: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft. Hit: 12 (2d6 + 5) bludgeoning. Target must make DC 14 Con save or become diseased. Diseased creature can't regain HP unless underwater. Disease removed only by Heal or 6th level+ cure magic.

Tail: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft. Hit: 15 (3d6 + 5) bludgeoning.

Enslave (3/Day): Target within 30 ft. must make DC 14 Wis save or be charmed. Charmed creature is under aboleth's control, can't take reactions. Charmed target can repeat save when taking damage or at 1 mile distance (once/24 hrs).

Legendary Actions (3 per round)

Detect: Makes a Wisdom (Perception) check.

Tail Swipe: Makes one tail attack.

Psychic Drain (Costs 2 Actions): One charmed creature takes 10 (3d6) psychic damage; aboleth regains HP equal to damage dealt.
