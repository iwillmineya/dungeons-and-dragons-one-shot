🏛️ Migdal-Or

A fortress city of stone and strength

← Back to Map

Description

Migdal-Or stands as a bastion of civilization—a great fortress city built upon ancient foundations. Its towering walls have protected countless generations, and its architecture reflects both martial prowess and artistic achievement. The city is a hub of military activity and serves as a vital stronghold for the region.

Notable Locations

The Great Citadel

The city's heart and seat of military command, impregnable and impressive.

Market District

A bustling area filled with vendors, craftspeople, and merchants from across the land.

Guard Barracks

Extensive military facilities housing the city's well-trained defenders.

NPCs & Encounters

War Commander

A veteran warrior who commands respect and loyalty from all soldiers.

Master Blacksmith

Creates the finest weapons and armor in the region.

City Guard

Well-equipped and disciplined soldiers patrolling the streets.

Adventure Hooks

  • Join the city guard on dangerous patrols
  • Investigate corruption within the military ranks
  • Commission custom weapons or armor
  • Defend the city from external threats
