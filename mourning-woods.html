Description

The Mourning Woods are a dense, ancient forest where sunlight struggles to penetrate the thick canopy. The woods earned their name from the sorrowful history that haunts them—a tragedy that transpired centuries ago. The forest is both beautiful and foreboding, filled with wildlife and magical creatures.

Notable Locations

Old Growth Forest

Trees so ancient they predate recorded history, creating a cathedral of wood and shadow.

Haunted Clearing

A mysterious clearing where strange phenomena occur at night.

Ranger Settlement

A hidden community of rangers and druids living in harmony with the forest.

NPCs & Encounters

Forest Spirits

Ethereal beings connected to the forest's ancient sorrow and tragedy.

Ranger Warden

Protects the forest and its inhabitants from those who would do harm.

Dangerous Beasts

Large predators and magical creatures roam the deep woods.

Adventure Hooks

  • Discover the truth behind the forest's tragic history
  • Rescue missing travelers lost in the woods
  • Investigate supernatural disturbances in the forest
  • Help the ranger settlement defend against poachers

Enemy/Encounter 1: Chimera

Type: Large Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil

CR: 6 (2,300 XP)

HP: 114 (12d10 + 48)

AC: 14 (natural armor)

Speed: 30 ft., fly 60 ft. | Proficiency Bonus: +3

Senses: Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 18 | Languages: Understands Draconic but can't speak

Ability Scores

STR 19 (+4) | DEX 11 (+0) | CON 19 (+4) | INT 3 (-4) | WIS 14 (+2) | CHA 10 (+0)

Skills: Perception +8

Actions

Multiattack: Makes three attacks: one bite, one horns, and one claws. Can use fire breath instead of bite or horns when available.

Bite: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing damage.

Horns: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 10 (1d12 + 4) bludgeoning damage.

Claws: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage.

Fire Breath (Recharge 5–6): Dragon head exhales fire in 15-foot cone. Each creature must make DC 15 Dex save, taking 31 (7d8) fire damage on failure or half on success.
