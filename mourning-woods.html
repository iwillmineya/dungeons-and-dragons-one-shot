🌲 Mourning Woods

A dark forest shrouded in sorrow and mystery

← Back to Map

Description

The Mourning Woods are a dense, ancient forest where sunlight struggles to penetrate the thick canopy. The woods earned their name from the sorrowful history that haunts them—a tragedy that transpired centuries ago. The forest is both beautiful and foreboding, filled with wildlife and magical creatures.

Notable Locations

Old Growth Forest

Trees so ancient they predate recorded history, creating a cathedral of wood and shadow.

Haunted Clearing

A mysterious clearing where strange phenomena occur at night.

Ranger Settlement

A hidden community of rangers and druids living in harmony with the forest.

NPCs & Encounters

Forest Spirits

Ethereal beings connected to the forest's ancient sorrow and tragedy.

Ranger Warden

Protects the forest and its inhabitants from those who would do harm.

Dangerous Beasts

Large predators and magical creatures roam the deep woods.

Adventure Hooks

  • Discover the truth behind the forest's tragic history
  • Rescue missing travelers lost in the woods
  • Investigate supernatural disturbances in the forest
  • Help the ranger settlement defend against poachers
