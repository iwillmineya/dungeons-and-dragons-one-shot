🌊 Great Sea of Tarshish

A vast ocean of mystery, danger, and adventure

← Back to Map

Description

The Great Sea of Tarshish is a vast expanse of ocean teeming with life, magic, and danger. Maritime expeditions traverse its waters seeking trade routes, treasure, and knowledge. Sailors speak in hushed tones of sea monsters, shipwrecks, and lost islands hidden within the endless blue.

Notable Locations

Open Waters

Vast stretches of sea where countless maritime expeditions have been lost.

Hidden Islands

Mysterious islands not found on most maps, said to hold ancient treasures.

Treacherous Straits

Narrow passages known for whirlpools, monsters, and unpredictable weather.

NPCs & Encounters

Sea Monsters

Legendary creatures of immense power and intelligence.

Merfolk

Sentient beings of the sea with their own complex society.

Ghost Ships

Cursed vessels eternally sailing the waters, harbingers of doom.

Adventure Hooks

  • Survive a naval voyage fraught with perils
  • Discover a hidden island and its secrets
  • Negotiate with merfolk or other sea folk
  • Lift a curse from a ghost ship
