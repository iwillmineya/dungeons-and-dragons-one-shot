⛵ Joppael

A coastal city of seafaring tradition and maritime commerce

← Back to Map

Description

Joppael is a thriving port city built on the coast, serving as a gateway to the seas. The city is home to seafarers, merchants, and adventurers. Ships of all nations dock here, making it a cosmopolitan hub of trade and culture. The sea provides both opportunity and danger.

Notable Locations

Great Harbor

A spacious port capable of handling numerous vessels, bustling with activity.

Sailor's Guild

The governing body of maritime affairs and crew employment.

Shipwright's Quarter

Where vessels are built, repaired, and maintained by skilled craftspeople.

NPCs & Encounters

Captain of the Guard

Commands the harbor's defenses against pirates and threats.

Ship Captains

Experienced sailors seeking crew for voyages across the seas.

Pirates & Smugglers

Operating in the shadows, always seeking opportunities.

Adventure Hooks

  • Join a ship's crew for a sea voyage
  • Stop pirates preying on merchant vessels
  • Investigate smuggling operations in the harbor
  • Search for sunken treasure in coastal waters
