Description

The Negev of Jubilee is a vast desert region renowned for its golden sands and hidden oases. Despite the harsh environment, it's home to resilient settlements and nomadic tribes. The desert holds many secrets, including ancient ruins and valuable resources hidden beneath the dunes.

Notable Locations

Hidden Oases

Precious water sources sustaining life in the desert, known to the local inhabitants.

Desert Ruins

Ancient structures buried in the sand, hints of civilizations long forgotten.

Nomadic Encampments

Mobile settlements of desert tribes living in harmony with the harsh environment.

NPCs & Encounters

Desert Tribes

Hardy nomads who know the desert's secrets and safe passages.

Tomb Raiders

Adventurers and thieves seeking treasures in ancient ruins.

Desert Creatures

Dangerous beasts and magical entities adapted to the harsh climate.

Adventure Hooks

  • Guide a lost caravan to safety
  • Explore ancient ruins and claim their treasures
  • Resolve conflicts between tribes
  • Survive encounters with desert predators
